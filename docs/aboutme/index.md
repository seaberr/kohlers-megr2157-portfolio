Hi I am Sebatian Kohler I am a sophomore mechanical engineering student at UNCC. My introduction to engineering came at a young age since my father was also a Mechanical Engineer. I learned a lot from him, and I would not be studying Engineering if not for him. He still helps me with my homework sometimes. I have previously worked as a lifeguard and an Air-cooled Porsche mechanic In high school and prior to University. I learned a lot from these two but more from the latter because as a mechanic you deal with other people's design choices every day> You have to deal with the Engineers mistakes or sometimes things they overlooked like serviceability. I enjoy working on cars, but I don't think I want to make it my career. It is not always the best Idea to turn your hobby into your job so I would like to work outside of that realm. I have spent my free time in the last 4 years restoring a 1977 Porsche 911s where I rebuilt the engine as well as painted and upholster the car: I left no bolt unturned. I know what it takes to get a project finished and I also know how hard it can be. I currently play Ice Hockey for Unc Charlotte and enjoy the learning that comes from being on a sports team. I purposefully try to engineer my life so that I am rounded so I don't get bored. Last summer I had the privilege of working for my dad where I did some reverse engineering work for Custom Braking Technologies where I worked on an aftermarket track brake for light rail just like the ones they use in Charlotte. My sister also is in Lee college of engineering studying Mechanical Engineering which I think is pretty cool.
